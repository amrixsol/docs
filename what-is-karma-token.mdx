## Current status

\$KARMA is live on Robinhood Chain, launched with Virtuals Protocol.

- Official contract: `0xB47f4702DEB124cb4eB6286bE83c9d84277C6239`
- Chain: Robinhood Chain (chain ID 4663)
- The legacy Solana token (`CmUnSFZCKUFGs2L8eZGQukAj833Y8r2SiJav55Nnrihd`) is deprecated. If you still hold it, migrate at [migrate.trykarma.app](https://migrate.trykarma.app).

In-app utility is live: holding \$KARMA unlocks fee rebates and reward multipliers (see [Holder Benefits](/holder-benefits)).

## Vision

Align platform performance with token holder upside, responsibly and compliantly.

## Live utility

- **[Holder benefits](/holder-benefits)**: holding \$KARMA unlocks KP multipliers (up to 3x) and swap-fee rebates up to 90%, paid in \$KARMA bought on the open market when you claim. Tiers are dollar-denominated and start at \$10. No staking, no lock-ups, no APY; benefits are product utility, not yield.
- **[Buybacks](/buybacks)**: live since July 2026. 95% of swap fee revenue buys \$KARMA on the open market on a randomized roughly-daily schedule, and 5% goes to charity. Holder rebate claims are themselves market buys delivered to the holder. Every buy is published with transaction links and public wallet addresses on [stats.trykarma.app](https://stats.trykarma.app); nothing is taken on trust.

## Planned utility (exploration)

- Higher card limits tied to holder tiers
- Governance signaling first, then formalization if appropriate

The above are not live today; details will be announced before launch.

## Not live yet

- No yield-bearing staking, liquidity mining, or rev-share distributions
- No governance portal
- Token not required to use current features (holding it only makes them cheaper)
