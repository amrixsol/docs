## Current status

- Official contract: `0xB47f4702DEB124cb4eB6286bE83c9d84277C6239`
- Chain: Robinhood Chain (chain ID 4663)
- The legacy Solana token (`CmUnSFZCKUFGs2L8eZGQukAj833Y8r2SiJav55Nnrihd`) is deprecated. If you still hold it, migrate at [migrate.trykarma.app](https://migrate.trykarma.app).

In-app utility is live: holding \$KARMA unlocks fee cashback and reward multipliers (see [Holder Benefits](/holder-benefits)).

## Vision

Align platform performance with token holder upside, responsibly and compliantly.

## Live utility

- **[Holder benefits](/holder-benefits)** — simply holding \$KARMA in your wallet unlocks KP multipliers (up to 3×) and swap-fee cashback in \$KARMA (up to 100% back). No staking, no lock-ups, no APY; benefits are product utility, not yield.
- **The buyback engine** — live since July 2026. Swap fees from across the platform are pooled and 95% buys \$KARMA on the open market, on a randomized roughly-daily schedule so the buys can't be front-run. Every buy is published with transaction links and public wallet addresses on our stats page; nothing is taken on trust.
- **Cashback buybacks** — every dollar of holder cashback we credit is bought back on the open market, turning holder trading fees into \$KARMA buy volume.

## Planned utility (exploration)

- Higher card limits tied to holder tiers
- Governance signaling first, then formalization if appropriate

The above are not live today; details will be announced before launch.

## Not live yet

- No yield-bearing staking, liquidity mining, or rev-share distributions
- No governance portal
- Token not required to use current features (holding it only makes them cheaper)
