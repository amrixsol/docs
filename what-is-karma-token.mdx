- Official contract: `0xB47f4702DEB124cb4eB6286bE83c9d84277C6239`
- Chain: Robinhood Chain (chain ID 4663)
- The legacy Solana token (`CmUnSFZCKUFGs2L8eZGQukAj833Y8r2SiJav55Nnrihd`) is deprecated. If you still hold it, migrate at [migrate.trykarma.app](https://migrate.trykarma.app).

In-app utility is live: holding \$KARMA unlocks fee rebates and reward multipliers (see [Holder Benefits](/holder-benefits)).

## Vision

Align platform performance with token holder upside, responsibly and compliantly.

## Live utility

- **[Holder benefits](/holder-benefits)**: holding \$KARMA unlocks KP multipliers (up to 3x) and swap-fee cashback up to 90%. Cashback used to buy \$KARMA counts as \$KARMA market buying; cashback paid in tokenized stocks does not. Tiers are dollar-denominated and start at \$10. No staking, no lock-ups, no APY; benefits are product utility, not yield.

## Published buyback policy

The [published policy](https://x.com/KarmaWallet/status/2110434397220393188) applies to fees **earned from October 16, 2026 at 00:00 UTC**. Eligible revenue is net in-app swap revenue after aggregator and direct routing costs; card, account, and perpetuals revenue are excluded.

Allocate **5% to charity first**, then **fully fund earned holder cashback**. Split only the remainder between additional \$KARMA buybacks and growth and operations:

| Fees earned from (00:00 UTC) | Additional buybacks / growth and operations |
| --- | --- |
| October 16, 2026 | 80% / 20% |
| November 16, 2026 | 75% / 25% |
| December 16, 2026 | 70% / 30% |
| January 16, 2027 | 65% / 35% |
| February 16, 2027 onward | 60% / 40% |

Each split applies until the next start date. Fees earned earlier retain the original 95% market-buying / 5% charity policy, even if collected or spent later. The existing split of ecosystem buyback tokens remains 50% ecosystem reserve and 50% creator and user incentives; holder cashback is separate. Stock cashback is not \$KARMA buying. There is no guaranteed buyback amount, price or return, and no burn commitment.

**Policy publication is verified; updated engine implementation and deployment are in progress and are not yet operationally verified.** See [Buybacks](/buybacks) for the full policy and [stats.trykarma.app](https://stats.trykarma.app) for recorded transactions and wallet links. Those records do not by themselves verify the updated allocation flow.
