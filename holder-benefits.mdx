Holding \$KARMA in your Karma wallet unlocks benefits automatically. There is nothing to deposit and nothing to lock: the app reads your balance and applies your tier on every trade and card purchase. Sell whenever you want.

Tiers are measured in the dollar value of the \$KARMA you hold, not a fixed token count. Fixed token thresholds break every time the price moves; dollar tiers stay stable through both directions.

## Tiers

| You hold | Fee rebate | KP multiplier | Also unlocks |
| -------- | ---------- | ------------- | ------------ |
| \$10+     | 10%        | 1.1x          | Holder status |
| \$100+    | 20%        | 1.25x         |              |
| \$500+    | 35%        | 1.5x          |              |
| \$2,500+  | 50%        | 2x            | 50% off card fees, Rising tier |
| \$5,000+  | 70%        | 2.5x          | 50% off card fees |
| \$10,000+ | 90%        | 3x            | Free card fees, Elite tier |

Entry starts at \$10 on purpose: almost anyone using the app has a reason to hold some.

## You are never downgraded because the price moved

The dollar figure is only used to qualify. The moment you reach a tier, the token amount you held is recorded, and you keep that tier for as long as you hold that many tokens or more. Price can do whatever it does. The only thing that moves you down a tier is selling.

In practice: qualify with \$10,000 of \$KARMA, and that tier is yours while you keep those tokens, whether they are worth \$6,000 or \$30,000 later.

Your balance is read as a 7 day average, so a tier cannot be bought with a single trade and sold the same day.

## Fee rebates, explained

You pay the normal swap fee like everyone else. Your tier's share of that fee accrues to you in dollars, and when you tap Claim on the Rewards screen, that amount is used to buy \$KARMA on the open market and send it straight to your wallet. Claims are available from \$1 accrued.

Example: a \$2,000 swap on Robinhood Chain pays the standard \$10 fee. At the \$10,000 tier, about \$8.95 comes back to you in market-bought \$KARMA. At the \$500 tier, about \$3.48.

The rebate is calculated on the fee minus a small execution minimum, the part that covers what your swap actually costs to process. On Robinhood Chain, Base, and Ethereum this is about \$0.05. On Solana it is \$0.45 when Karma sponsors your gas (the default), or \$0.05 when you pay your own gas; keep a little SOL in your wallet and the app switches you automatically.

Why 90% at the top and not 100%: a fully waived fee is a buyback that never happens. At 90%, every trade at every tier keeps buying \$KARMA on the market, however large the trader gets. See [Buybacks](/buybacks) for how the whole engine fits together.

## What the KP multiplier applies to

Karma Points earned on swaps and card purchases. KP determine your rewards tier (Seed / Rising / Elite / Black) and its cashback rate, so a higher multiplier compounds: you earn KP faster, climb tiers sooner, and your rewards grow with you.

Holding \$2,500+ also unlocks the Rising tier directly, and \$10,000+ unlocks Elite, even before your KP would get you there. Black stays earn-only: it cannot be bought with holdings.

## Card fees

Card benefits are an instant discount rather than a rebate: \$2,500+ holders pay half the Karma service fee on card purchases, \$10,000+ holders pay none. The discount is applied at authorization, so the saving shows up on the receipt, not later.

## What holder benefits are *not*

- **Not a yield product.** No APY, no emissions, no payouts for parking tokens. A rebate returns part of a fee you actually paid, in proportion to your own trading.
- **Not a lock-up.** Your tokens never leave your wallet. Sell any time; your tier adjusts by the rules above.
- **Not required.** Every Karma feature works without holding \$KARMA. Holding only makes it cheaper.
