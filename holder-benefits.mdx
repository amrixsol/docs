Holding \$KARMA in your Karma wallet unlocks benefits automatically. There is nothing to deposit and nothing to lock: the app reads your balance and applies your tier on every trade and card purchase. Sell whenever you want.

Staking is retired. It used to require moving tokens into a vault with a 7-day exit cooldown. Holder benefits replace it with something strictly better: the same perks (and bigger ones), triggered by simply holding.

## Tiers

| \$KARMA held | KP multiplier | Swap-fee cashback | Card fees | Rewards tier unlock |
| ----------- | ------------- | ----------------- | --------- | ------------------- |
| 100,000+    | 1.5×          | —                 | —         | —                   |
| 1,000,000+  | 2×            | 50% back          | 50% off   | Rising              |
| 10,000,000+ | 3×            | 100% back         | Free      | Elite               |

Your balance is counted across all your Karma wallets and updates automatically when tokens move in or out. Black tier (25% cashback) remains earn-only and cannot be unlocked by holding.

## Fee cashback, explained

You pay the normal swap fee like everyone else. After the trade, your tier's share of that fee comes back to you as \$KARMA, claimable in the app from the Rewards screen. Claimed tokens land in your wallet, where they immediately count toward your tier.

The cashback is calculated on the fee minus a small network minimum that covers what your swap actually costs to process:

- **\$0.45** when Karma sponsors your network gas (the default).
- **\$0.05** when you pay your own gas. Keep a little SOL in your wallet and the app switches you to this automatically. Gas costs you around one cent, and your cashback grows by \$0.40 per trade.

Example: a \$2,000 swap pays the standard \$10 fee. A 10M holder with SOL in their wallet gets \$9.95 back in \$KARMA. A 1M holder gets about \$4.98 back.

Every dollar of cashback we credit is bought back on the open market. Holder fees do not sit in a treasury: they become \$KARMA buy volume, and then they become yours again.

## What the KP multiplier applies to

Karma Points earned on swaps and card purchases. KP determine your rewards tier (Seed / Rising / Elite / Black) and its cashback rate, so a higher multiplier compounds: you earn KP faster, climb tiers sooner, and the tier cashback on card spend grows with you.

Holding 1M+ also unlocks the Rising tier directly, and 10M+ unlocks Elite, even before your KP would get you there.

## Card fees

Card fee benefits are an instant discount rather than cashback: 1M+ holders pay half the Karma service fee on card purchases, 10M+ holders pay none. The discount is applied at authorization, so the saving shows up on the receipt, not later.

## If you still have staked \$KARMA

Legacy staked balances keep counting toward your tiers, so nothing breaks. You can withdraw them to your wallet from the Rewards screen whenever you like (the old vault's 7-day cooldown still applies to the withdrawal itself). Once withdrawn, the tokens count the same as any held \$KARMA. New staking deposits are no longer possible.

## What holder benefits are *not*

- **Not a yield product.** No APY, no emissions, no payouts for parking tokens. Cashback is a rebate on fees you actually paid, in proportion to your own trading.
- **Not a lock-up.** Your tokens never leave your wallet. Sell any time; your tier simply adjusts.
- **Not required.** Every Karma feature works without holding \$KARMA. Holding only makes it cheaper.
