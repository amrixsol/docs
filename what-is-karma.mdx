Karma is a self-custodial crypto super app: trade tokens across four chains, spend your balance with a card, bank with real account numbers, and share your trades with people who care. One app, one balance, your keys.

We started from a simple observation: the tools people actually use every day (trading, spending, banking, talking about it) live in five different apps that do not know about each other. Karma puts them in one place without taking custody of your money.

## What Karma does

- **Trade**: Solana, Base, Ethereum, and Robinhood Chain (including tokenized stocks) with one-tap buys funded from a single cash balance, gasless on the default routes, with limit orders and scam protection built in.
- **Spend**: a card funded straight from your balance, with instant notifications and holder discounts.
- **Bank**: real EUR IBANs, US account numbers, and GBP accounts in your name, delivering to your wallet as digital dollars. On-ramp and off-ramp in USD, EUR, or GBP.
- **Share**: a feed where your trades can post themselves with real P&L, a leaderboard, and profiles with honest per-trade history.
- **Hold \$KARMA**: fee rebates up to 90% and multiplied rewards, funded by real revenue. See [What is \$KARMA](/what-is-karma-token).

The full feature tour lives on the [Features](/features) page.

## Earn Good Karma

A portion of our fees goes to vetted charities. On every transaction you complete, part of what we earn is directed to impact. Your balances are never reduced for donations; the contribution comes from us.

## Self-custody, for real

Wallets are secured by your passkey. We cannot move your funds, you can export your keys at any time, and every fiat rail delivers to a wallet you control. Regulated partners provide the banking and card services; you provide the keys.

<Note>
  Karma Fintech is a financial technology company, not a bank. Banking services and issued cards (where applicable) are provided by regulated partners.
</Note>
