Karma's buyback policy links spending to eligible net in-app swap revenue. The [published policy](https://x.com/KarmaWallet/status/2110434397220393188) applies to fees **earned from October 16, 2026 at 00:00 UTC**, after aggregator and direct routing costs. Card, account, and perpetuals revenue are excluded.

**Publication is verified. Implementation and deployment of the updated allocation engine are in progress; the updated flow is not yet operationally verified.** Wallet links and recorded buys on [stats.trykarma.app](https://stats.trykarma.app) are transaction evidence, not proof that the updated allocation policy has been deployed or reconciled.

## Where eligible net swap revenue goes

1. **Charity first:** allocate 5% of eligible net in-app swap revenue to charity.
2. **Earned holder cashback next:** fully fund earned holder cashback from the remaining revenue before allocating any additional buyback or growth budget.
3. **Split the remainder:** allocate what remains after charity and earned cashback to additional \$KARMA buybacks and growth and operations, according to the schedule below.

| Fees earned from (00:00 UTC) | Additional buybacks | Growth and operations |
| --- | --- | --- |
| October 16, 2026 | 80% of the remainder | 20% of the remainder |
| November 16, 2026 | 75% of the remainder | 25% of the remainder |
| December 16, 2026 | 70% of the remainder | 30% of the remainder |
| January 16, 2027 | 65% of the remainder | 35% of the remainder |
| February 16, 2027 onward | 60% of the remainder | 40% of the remainder |

Each row applies until the next start date. The applicable policy and split follow **when the fee was earned**, not when it was collected, swept, claimed, or spent. The percentages in the table apply only to the remainder, not to gross fees or all company revenue.

## Earlier fees keep the original policy

Fees earned before October 16, 2026 at 00:00 UTC retain the original policy: 95% for \$KARMA market buying and 5% for charity. The original launch window was July 18 through October 15, 2026. Settling those fees later does not move them into the updated allocation schedule.

## Cashback and bought KARMA

Earned cashback must be fully funded before the remainder is split. Only cashback actually used to buy \$KARMA counts as \$KARMA market buying. Cashback paid in tokenized stocks is not \$KARMA buying, and funding a cashback liability is not itself a completed buy.

The existing destination split for \$KARMA bought for the ecosystem is preserved: **50% to the ecosystem reserve and 50% to creator and user incentives**. Holder cashback is a separate allocation to eligible holders. The ecosystem reserve never sells, but may distribute with notice, for example through airdrops or incentives. Incentive tokens may re-enter circulation.

## What this does not promise

- No guaranteed buyback amount, cadence, token price, or return. Additional buybacks depend on eligible revenue remaining after charity and earned cashback.
- No burn commitment. Bought tokens are allocated to the stated destinations, not automatically destroyed or permanently removed from circulation.
- Publication does not establish operational execution. Implementation, deployment, and reconciliation of the updated engine still need verification.
