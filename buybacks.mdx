Karma's token economy runs on real revenue, not emissions. Swap fees from across the platform are pooled, and 95% buys \$KARMA on the open market while 5% goes to charity. This policy runs for 90 days from launch (July 18 through October 15, 2026) and will then be publicly reviewed; any change is announced before it happens, never retroactively.

Every step is verifiable: wallet addresses are public, every buy has a transaction link, and the live totals are on [stats.trykarma.app](https://stats.trykarma.app).

## Where every dollar goes

1. **Swap fees** from Solana, Base, Ethereum, and Robinhood Chain consolidate into a single fee wallet.
2. **5% goes to charity**, paid out monthly with published receipts.
3. **95% buys \$KARMA on the market.** The buys run on a randomized roughly-daily schedule so they cannot be front-run.
4. Bought \$KARMA splits between an **ecosystem reserve** (which never sells) and **creator and user incentives**.

## Holder rebates are buybacks too

When a holder claims a fee rebate, that claim is executed as a market buy of \$KARMA delivered straight to their wallet. Rebates do not reduce the buyback total: they redirect a slice of the same 95% from the reserve to the trading holder. Total market buying stays at 95% of every fee, at every tier. The tier only decides who ends up holding the bought \$KARMA.

This is also why the top rebate tier is 90% and not 100%: a fully waived fee would be a buyback that never happens.

## What this is not

- Not a promise that buybacks run forever. The policy has a stated window and a public review date.
- Not a price guarantee. Buybacks are a use of revenue, not a floor.
- The reserve never sells, but it may distribute (for example airdrops or incentives) with notice.
