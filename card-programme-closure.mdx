The Karma card programme closed on **5 Nov 2026**. New cards, top-ups, and card spending are no longer available.

## Existing balances and transactions

Open **Cards** in the Karma app for recovery and transaction history. Check the available balance and the destination wallet shown before confirming a withdrawal.

Closure does not mean that a balance has already moved, or that pending holds, refunds, or disputes have settled. If a balance or withdrawal is unavailable, [contact support](/support).

EUR, GBP, and USD virtual accounts are unchanged by this closure.

## Previous terms

The [card programme closure notice and archived terms](https://trykarma.app/card-terms) remain available on the Karma website.
