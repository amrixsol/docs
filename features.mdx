Karma is a self-custodial money app. You trade tokens across three chains from one cash balance, spend with a card, bank with real account numbers, and share your trades with people who care. Your keys stay yours the whole time.

## Trading

- **Three chains, one app** — trade on Solana, Base, and Robinhood Chain with the same one-tap flow. Positions, charts, and history understand every chain.
- **One cash balance** — buys fund themselves from wherever your money sits. You never think about which chain your cash is on.
- **Gasless** — network fees are sponsored on the default routes. No SOL or ETH babysitting required.
- **Limit orders** — take-profit, stop-loss, and limit buys on Solana tokens.
- **Launches** — a launchpad tab in Explore for tokens launching on Robinhood Chain, with creator track records, locked-liquidity info, and a Graduated tab for the ones that made it.
- **Scam protection** — before you buy, Karma verifies the token can actually be sold and screens new tokens against reputation data. Malicious tokens are blocklisted app-wide.
- **Stock and ETF tokens** — tokenized stocks on Robinhood Chain with real company logos and charts drawn from live market data.

## Wallets

- **Self-custody, always** — wallets are secured by your passkey. We cannot move your funds, and you can export your keys at any time.
- **Import any wallet** — bring an existing wallet by seed phrase or private key, Solana or Ethereum, and get your full experience on it: balances, trading, history, and P&L tracked from the day you import.
- **Send anywhere** — send tokens or cash to any address, with wrong-chain guardrails that catch mistakes before they cost you.

## Cards

- **Spend your balance** — pay anywhere cards are accepted, funded straight from your Karma balance.
- **Instant notifications** — every authorization, decline, and refund lands as a push the moment it happens.
- **Holder discounts** — \$KARMA holders pay half or zero service fees depending on tier (see [Holder Benefits](/holder-benefits)).

## Banking

- **EUR IBANs, US account numbers, GBP accounts** — real account details in your name, delivering to your wallet as digital dollars.
- **Deposit and withdraw** — on-ramp by bank transfer, off-ramp to your own bank accounts in USD, EUR, or GBP.
- **Send in the recipient's currency** — bank sends are denominated in what the recipient receives, with fees shown on top instead of quietly deducted.

## Social

- **Feed** — post your takes with tokens attached, and your trades can post themselves with real position P&L. Repost, comment (GIFs included), and follow traders you rate.
- **Leaderboard** — top traders by realized P&L across 24H, 1W, 1M, and all-time windows.
- **Profiles** — open positions with honest per-round-trip P&L, verified badges, and a linked X account if you connect one.

## Rewards

- **Karma Points** — earn KP on swaps and card spend, climb tiers, unlock cashback.
- **Holder benefits** — holding \$KARMA multiplies your KP and returns up to 100% of swap fees as \$KARMA. No staking, no lock-ups. Full breakdown on [Holder Benefits](/holder-benefits).
- **Referrals** — invite friends with your own custom invite code.

## Compliance & security

- **Regulated partners** — fiat rails run through regulated partners with full KYC.
- **Self custody** — we do not take control of user keys, and account removal works without us in the loop.
