## Simple pricing

### Card payments
- **Karma service fee**: 0.5% per transaction
- **FX fee**: 1% on non-USD transactions
- **International transaction fee**: \$0.30 flat network fee charged by Visa on non-USD transactions

### Bank transfers
- **On-ramp fee**: 1%\*
- **Off-ramp fee**: 0.5%
- **SEPA transfer**: \$1.45
- **ACH transfer**: \$0.95
- **FX fee**: Mid-market + 0.50% on EUR, GBP, and MXN conversions

### Trading (Solana token swaps)
Karma routes swaps through Jupiter and aggregates the best price across Solana DEXes. We charge a single in-app fee on top — no spread markup, and no separate Jupiter platform fee on your end.

- **Karma swap fee**: 0.5% of trade size
  - **Minimum $0.45** per trade (floor for very small trades)
- **Network gas**: **Sponsored by Karma** on the default route (Jupiter Ultra, gasless). Only the legacy v6 fallback — used in rare edge cases — passes Solana network gas (typically under \$0.01) on to you.
- **DEX/LP fees**: Set by the underlying liquidity venue (Raydium, Meteora, Orca, etc.) and already reflected in the price you see. We do not add a markup on top.

#### Holder cashback

\$KARMA holders get part or all of the Karma swap fee back as \$KARMA, claimable in the app. Everyone pays the same fee at trade time; the cashback comes back after the swap and is calculated on the fee minus a small network minimum (\$0.45 when Karma sponsors your gas, \$0.05 when you pay your own).

| \$KARMA held | Swap-fee cashback | Card service fee |
| ----------- | ----------------- | ---------------- |
| < 1M        | —                 | full fee         |
| 1M+         | 50% back          | 50% off          |
| 10M+        | 100% back         | free             |

See the [Holder Benefits](/holder-benefits) page for the full breakdown.

### General
- **No subscriptions**: No signup, monthly, or hidden fees

Rates are fetched live and displayed in app.

\*Additional fees may apply for third-party deposits, high-risk transactions, or enhanced due diligence requirements. For high-risk payments, this additional fee is capped at 5% of the deposit amount.
