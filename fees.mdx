## Simple pricing

### Card payments
- **Karma service fee**: 0.5% per transaction
- **FX fee**: 1% on non-USD transactions
- **International transaction fee**: \$0.30 flat network fee charged by Visa on non-USD transactions

### Bank transfers
- **On-ramp fee**: 1%\*
- **Off-ramp fee**: 0.5%
- **SEPA transfer**: \$1.45
- **ACH transfer**: \$0.95
- **FX fee**: Mid-market + 0.50% on EUR, GBP, and MXN conversions

### Trading (Solana token swaps)
Karma aggregates the best available price across Solana liquidity venues. We charge a single in-app fee on top, with no spread markup.

- **Karma swap fee**: 0.5% of trade size
  - **Minimum $0.45** per trade on trades of \$2 or more (trades under \$2 pay the flat 0.5%)
- **Network gas**: **Sponsored by Karma** on the default route (gasless). Rare fallback routes pass Solana network gas (typically under \$0.01) on to you.
- **DEX/LP fees**: Set by the underlying liquidity venue and already reflected in the price you see. We do not add a markup on top.

### Trading (Base and Robinhood Chain token swaps)
Same simple pricing:

- **Karma swap fee**: 0.5% of trade size
  - **Robinhood Chain**: same **\$0.45 minimum** as Solana on trades of \$2 or more (trades under \$2 pay the flat 0.5%)
  - **Base**: no minimum, even on the smallest trades
- **Network gas**: **Sponsored by Karma** — swaps execute gasless, so you never need ETH in your wallet to trade
- **Cash conversions are free**: moving between stablecoins (for example USDC ↔ USDG) carries no Karma fee
- **DEX/LP fees**: Set by the underlying liquidity venue and already reflected in the price you see. We do not add a markup on top.

Cross-chain swaps (for example funding a Base or Robinhood Chain trade from your Solana balance) follow the Solana pricing above: 0.5% with the \$0.45 minimum on trades of \$2 or more.

#### Holder cashback

\$KARMA holders get part or all of the Karma swap fee back as \$KARMA, claimable in the app. Everyone pays the same fee at trade time; the cashback comes back after the swap and is calculated on the fee minus a small network minimum (\$0.45 when Karma sponsors your gas, \$0.05 when you pay your own).

| \$KARMA held | Swap-fee cashback | Card service fee |
| ----------- | ----------------- | ---------------- |
| < 1M        | —                 | full fee         |
| 1M+         | 50% back          | 50% off          |
| 10M+        | 100% back         | free             |

See the [Holder Benefits](/holder-benefits) page for the full breakdown.

### General
- **No subscriptions**: No signup, monthly, or hidden fees

Rates are fetched live and displayed in app.

\*Additional fees may apply for third-party deposits, high-risk transactions, or enhanced due diligence requirements. For high-risk payments, this additional fee is capped at 5% of the deposit amount.
